KARIA V7.49 — Noir store look + full audit fixes (2026-11-21)

LOOK
- New "Noir Atelier" look: near-black, ivory and gold; Bodoni Moda + Jost.
- New home (src/noir.jsx): crossfade hero, 3D coverflow of every bag (available first), the craft story, and the making-of film (loads only when it is on screen).
- The original look is kept. To switch: Admin → Settings → Store look → Classic.
  It is also saved on GitHub as branch "estilo-original" and tag "estilo-original-v7.48".

STORE FIXES
- Phone header: the bag, account and search icons were invisible. My orders and Account were added to the phone menu.
- New pages: Shipping, Returns & Refunds, Privacy and Terms (a draft; have them reviewed). Linked from the footer and the checkout.
- Shop sorts available bags first. "You may also like" shows available bags first. Empty stars are hidden.
- The bag refreshes prices and stock from the store and drops sold-out pieces. It says that shipping is added at checkout.
- Checkout: sign-in notice at the top, a message after a canceled payment, links to the policies.
- The success page checks that the order is really paid.
- Forgot password. The confirmation email returns to the checkout.
- No more demo bags with fake prices while loading. Real 404 page.
- A title on every page. Description, Open Graph image, favicon, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
- Price font is readable. Inputs use the store font and are 16px on phones. Visible focus. Dialogs close with Esc.
- Lighter: the admin loads only for admins, the films no longer autoplay-download, card photos use thumbnails, unused packages and the 9.5 MB unused video are removed.

ADMIN FIXES (src/admin.jsx + src/admin.css)
- Products:
  - Initial stock when creating a product.
  - Photo manager in Edit: add, delete, reorder, make main.
  - Photos are resized before upload.
  - No half-created products when an upload fails.
  - The "Publish" checkbox works.
  - Visible labels and an automatic SKU.
  - Stock column, + STOCK, DUPLICATE and search.
- Profit formula fixed: tax and shipping were subtracted twice. Same formula on Dashboard, Accounting and each order.
- Inventory is classified by movement type. Sale movements are locked. Deleting a test order returns its stock.
- Order status changes are limited to sensible ones. Cancelled/refunded orders return stock (SQL trigger).
- Coupons: max uses, edit, end of day. Expenses: edit and delete. Orders: filters and search. Brands: edit and activate.
- Settings: Store look. Notices show at the bottom on phones.
