KARIA V7.9 ACCOUNTING
- Production and Materials removed from the Admin navigation/UI.
- Accounting becomes the financial center.
- Paid order items are recorded automatically in the Sales Ledger.
- Pending orders are excluded.
- Columns: date, order, product, description, qty, unit cost, sale price, sale total, total cost, gross profit, gross margin %, markup %.
- KPI cards: sales revenue, COGS, gross profit, gross margin, operating expenses, net profit.
- Historical profitability uses order_items.unit_cost snapshot.
- Existing expense entry remains and is integrated into net profit.
- Responsive accounting table for mobile.
- Existing Inventory, Orders, Shop, Checkout and Super Admin functionality retained.
No SQL migration required for this UI/reporting update.
