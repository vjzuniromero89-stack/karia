KARIA V7.10
Accounting sale ledger rebuilt.

For every PAID sale line:
- Product / description / qty
- Unit cost
- Unit sale price
- Product total
- Discount
- Net product sale
- Shipping deducted
- Tax deducted
- Customer total
- Product cost / COGS
- Payment processing fee
- Final profit
- Profit percentage

Formula:
Final profit = net product sale - product cost - shipping - tax - processor fee.
Profit % = final profit / net product sale * 100.

Order-level discount/shipping/tax/processor fee are allocated proportionally across products when an order contains multiple items.
Pending orders remain excluded.
Operating expenses are shown separately and reduce overall net profit.
No SQL migration required.
